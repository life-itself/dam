# SCQH on Digital Asset Management

## SITUATION

Life Itself is undergoing a major website redesign, including a new dedicated site for its Hubs, a refreshed Life Itself homepage, and work on the Second Renaissance platform. Alongside this, there's a growing need for high-quality visual content—images, videos, and other media—for these sites, as well as for social media and promotional use. Over the years, Life Itself has accumulated a large volume of raw content from events and activities, including thousands of photos and dozens (hundreds?) of videos. Some infrastructure is in place, such as a content log for meetings and recordings, and the team already uses platforms like YouTube and Google Drive.

## COMPLICATION

There is currently no central, organized repository for digital assets. Content is scattered across platforms (YouTube, Google Drive, etc.), with little consistency in where new material is saved or how it is tagged. While some images and videos are already in use, there is no systematic way to track their origin, quality, or frequency of use. This leads to:

* Repetitive or suboptimal content being used in public-facing materials.  
* Uncertainty about what good-quality material exists.  
* High cognitive and time burdens for team members trying to locate or curate assets.

Additionally, expecting people to tag, label, or curate raw content **at the time of capture is unrealistic** due to limited capacity. Curation tends to happen passively when assets are used, but that usage is not currently tracked. There is also no clear infrastructure for surfacing “top assets” or enabling reuse with minimal friction.

## QUESTION

**How should Life Itself manage its digital assets to support immediate content needs while laying the groundwork for sustainable long-term curation?**

### Sub-questions:

1. **Where are our content lakes?** What platforms are currently in use for raw storage (e.g. Drive, YouTube)?  
2. **What is our minimal metadata standard?** How do we consistently capture core info like date, location, people, and context?  
3. **How can we identify high-quality assets?** What light-touch or automated ways can flag good material (e.g. based on frequency of use)?  
4. **How do we support immediate needs?** What material should be curated right now for the Hubs website, Life Itself homepage, and blog/social media?  
5. **How do we integrate back-end and front-end workflows?** Can usage (e.g. in a blog post) automatically surface an asset as "curated"?
