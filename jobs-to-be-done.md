# Jobs To Be Done

## 1. Find the right media for live website and comms needs

When we are building or updating a page, campaign, or social post, we need to quickly find strong, relevant images and video so that publishing work does not stall on asset hunting.

## 2. Build a usable archive, not just a storage dump

When we gather raw media into a shared archive, we want it to become a usable background resource that people can browse, search, and return to later, not just a place where files go to disappear.

## 3. Surface underexploited material for future reuse and refinement

When we review our existing photo and video archive, we want to know what valuable material has been underused, unpublished, insufficiently promoted, or never developed into a fuller output so that we can turn it into future films, edits, stories, B-roll collections, or other content.

This includes both:

- unpublished raw material that has never been turned into an output
- published material that exists already but has not been properly reused, promoted, remixed, or surfaced again
- strong A-roll as well as B-roll and other supporting footage
