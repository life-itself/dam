## Overview

We have a growing need to better **organize and surface our digital content**—especially images and videos—for our new websites. A structured approach to managing this content will also support ongoing communications, social media, and storytelling work.

## Immediate Focus

* **High priority** is collecting relevant **raw image and video material** for the new websites (e.g. the Hubs site, Orion site, Second Renaissance).  
* We’re exploring **two parallel approaches**:  
  1. **Content Lake** approach: systematically **dumping all existing material** into a central repository for future curation and AI-assisted processing.  
  2. **Page-led (pull)** approach: identify what pages we’re building now, and **pull together the best existing material** to support those pages.

Right now, the **pull approach is most urgent**, as we need content for specific pages that are actively being built.

### Roles

* **Surfacing relevant content** (esp. for the Hubs site first),  
* And helping us **organize or centralize the archive** (content lake) long-term.

## Questions for Exploration

* **Where is the content currently stored?** E.g., Google Drive, YouTube, Google Photos, Canva.  
* **What is our main “content lake”?** At present, probably Google Drive—though Google Photos may be better for some things (e.g. albums, visual browsing).  
* **How can we tag or flag content easily?** Even informal systems like album names, shared docs, or lightweight tagging may help.  
* **Can AI assist in organizing over time?** We expect to rely more on AI curation going forward, so dumping now and organizing later is acceptable.
